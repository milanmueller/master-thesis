\section*{Declaration of AI Usage}

\bigskip
\bigskip
\bigskip

I hereby declare that, with the approval of my examiner, I have
employed the generative AI tool Claude Code by Anthropic
during the preparation of this thesis.

I used Claude Code to write parts of the Isabelle proofs and definitions
and to debug the implementation and benchmark setup.
I also used it to detect syntactic and semantic issues in the written thesis
and to generate custom scripts for evaluation of the Isabelle implementation
and visual presentation of the experimental results.

I expressly confirm that AI tools were not used as a
source of factual information for this thesis.
