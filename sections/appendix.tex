\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\emph{Refinement infrastructure}} \\
\isaname{Aux_Lemmas} & new & Lemmas and definitions from Imperative-HOL that Isabelle-LLVM lacks (\isaname{list_mset_rel}, \isaname{monadic_nfoldli}). \\
\isaname{LLVM_Assn_Env} & new & Hierarchy of refinement assertions, discussed in \ref{sec:interface-hierarchy-for-refinement-assertions}.\\
\isaname{More_Loops} & AFP & Unchanged. \\
\isaname{More_EOArray} & new & An extension to the \isaname{Isabelle_LLVM.EOArray} theory. \\
\isaname{PAC_Map_Rel} & AFP & Ported to Isabelle-LLVM; removed the rewrite of key membership into an option-returning lookup; commented out the Imperative-HOL hash map. \\
\isaname{PAC_Checker_Relation} & AFP & Largely rewritten: removed the \isaname{String.literal} representation and the assertions for monomials, polynomials, and steps (now in \isaname{PAC_Polynomials_Assn} and \isaname{PAC_Step_Assn}); kept the term order; added \isaname{mnl_le_impl}. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\emph{Data structures}} \\
\isaname{LLVM_DS_Copying_List} & new & Copying list \isaname{cl_assn}: a singly linked list whose head operation copies the element instead of destroying the list; includes non-destructive folds with an explicit environment, because LLVM has no closures. \\
\isaname{LLVM_DS_Partial_Map} & new & Partial map from 64-bit words to values with possibly non-pure refinement assertions over an initialised array list (\isaname{pmap_assn}); boxing (\isaname{box_assn}) to store such values. \\
\isaname{LLVM_DS_Hash_Set} & new & Hash set (\isaname{hs_assn}) of elements with possibly non-pure refinement assertions, with copying-list buckets and resizing. \\
\isaname{LLVM_DS_Hash_Map} & new & Hash map with copying-list buckets of key and boxed value; reuses the bucket machinery of the hash set. \\
\isaname{LLVM_DS_Char} & new & Refinement of \isaname{char} to 8-bit words, linear order and comparisons on characters, FNV-1a hashing. \\
\isaname{LLVM_DS_String} & new & List-based (\isaname{strl_assn}) and array-based (\isaname{stra_assn}) strings with hashing, equality, lexicographic order, and conversion from list to array. \\
\isaname{LLVM_ASCII_String} & new & Parsing of decimal ASCII strings into signed big integers and printing of big integers to strings. \\
\isaname{LLVM_List_Sorting} & new & Generic bottom-up merge sort over copying lists; replaces \isaname{WB_Sort} of the AFP. \\
\isaname{LLVM_Interleaving_Fold} & new & Fold over two copying lists at once (\isaname{ifoldl}, \isaname{cl_ifoldl}) that advances the left, right, or both lists per step; used for merging without copying. Not listed in \isaname{ROOT}; imported. \\
\isaname{PAC_Polynomials_Assn} & new & Assertions for monomials, coefficients, and polynomials over copying lists, list-based strings, and big integers; copy, free, comparison, and printing. \\
\isaname{PAC_Polynomials_Assn_Array} & new & Variant of \isaname{PAC_Polynomials_Assn} with array-based strings, as produced by the C parser; normalisation of polynomials. Not listed in \isaname{ROOT}; imported. \\
\isaname{LLVM_DS_Char_Literals} & new & Constant table that lets Sepref synthesise character literals (printable ASCII and newline). \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\emph{Polynomial layer}} \\
\isaname{PAC_Polynomials} & AFP & Unchanged. \\
\isaname{PAC_Polynomials_Term} & AFP & Moved the order on characters to \isaname{LLVM_DS_Char}. \\
\isaname{PAC_Polynomials_Operations} & AFP & Replaced the recursive \isaname{add_poly_l} by a curried loop version with an equivalence proof; added a loop version of coefficient merging (\isaname{merge_coeffs1}). \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\emph{Checker layer}} \\
\isaname{PAC_Checker} & AFP & Ported the step assertion to Isabelle-LLVM separation logic; adapted to the curried \isaname{add_poly_l}; adapted the overflow bound to \isaname{max_snat 64}. \\
\isaname{PAC_Step_Assn} & new & Assertion for PAC steps as tagged products, with constructors, discriminators, destructors, and deallocation. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Step_Assn} & new & Assertion for LPAC steps, analogous to \isaname{PAC_Step_Assn}; replaces \isaname{LPAC_Steps_Refine} of IsaFoL. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Step_Assn_Array} & new & Variant of \isaname{LPAC_Step_Assn} with array-based strings. Not listed in \isaname{ROOT}; imported. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Checker} & IsaFoL & Replaced the Imperative-HOL step assertion by \isaname{LPAC_Step_Assn}; adapted to the curried \isaname{add_poly_l}. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Checker_Error} & new & Synthesis of the LPAC error messages for both checkers and printing of shared polynomials; moved out of \isaname{LPAC_Checker_Synthesis} and \isaname{LPAC_Efficient_Checker_Synthesis}. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Perfectly_Shared} & IsaFoL & Unchanged apart from imports. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Perfectly_Shared_Vars} & IsaFoL & Kept the abstract layer; replaced the Imperative-HOL implementation by an Isabelle-LLVM one (string table and hash map, memory status as 1-bit word). \\
\isaname{LPAC_Efficient_Checker} & IsaFoL & Adapted to the curried \isaname{add_poly_l}. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Efficient_Checker_Sorting} & new & Merge sort with a monadic comparison, needed because the order on shared terms depends on the variable table. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Version} & IsaFoL & Unchanged. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\emph{Synthesis}} \\
\isaname{PAC_Checker_Error} & new & Synthesis of the status type and the PAC error messages; moved out of \isaname{PAC_Checker_Synthesis} of the AFP. \\
\isaname{PAC_Checker_Init} & AFP & Reduced to the normalisation of input polynomials; replaced quicksort and \isaname{WB_Sort} by \isaname{LLVM_List_Sorting}. \\
\isaname{PAC_Checker_Synthesis} & AFP & Restructured: hash set of strings for variables, partial map for polynomials, loop and fold refinements of polynomial arithmetic, new side condition \isaname{step_id_bounded}; removed code export; correctness theorem commented out. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Checker_Init} & IsaFoL & Emptied; the sorting it contained is no longer needed. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Efficient_Checker_Refinement} & new & Refinement layer of the efficient checker (nres programs and their refinement proofs, without Sepref); moved out of \isaname{LPAC_Efficient_Checker_Synthesis} and reworked for synthesis. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Efficient_Checker_Synthesis} & IsaFoL & Reduced to Sepref synthesis of the efficient checker; added fused lookup and addition that avoids copying polynomials from the map. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Checker_Synthesis} & IsaFoL & Rewritten for Isabelle-LLVM; added \isaname{step_id_bounded}; removed SML-specific code setup and code export. \\
\isaname{LPAC_Codegen} & new & Export of LLVM code and C header: builder functions for the C parser and the entry point \isaname{run_checker}; replaces \isaname{PAC_Checker_MLton}, \isaname{LPAC_Checker_MLton}, and \isaname{LPAC_Efficient_Checker_MLton}. \\
